The heading outline now opens as a full screen of its own, from its own button in the editor toolbar, with every entry drawn at one size and indentation showing the level. Tapping a heading while editing moves the cursor there instead of switching to reading view. Settings adds Open notes at: Top, Bottom, or Where I left off, with Top still the default; positions are kept in the app, never in your files. Long notes also gain a button that jumps to the other end.
