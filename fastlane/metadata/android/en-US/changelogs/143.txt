Tables and callouts are now in the formatting panel.

- The panel above the keyboard reaches two formats it used to leave out: tables and > [!NOTE] callouts. Inserting either no longer means typing the Markdown by hand.
- A table goes in as its own block, so it renders as a table even when you tap the button in the middle of a paragraph.
- Tap Callout with text selected and that text becomes the callout.
