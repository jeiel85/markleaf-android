Sync can now keep its bookkeeping beside your files instead of inside them. Settings > Multi-device sync > Note metadata moves the small --- header out of each note into a hidden index, so your .md files hold only what you wrote. Switching converts the folder both ways and never alters your text. Two costs: renaming a file outside Markleaf can break its link, and a folder copied without its index loses created dates and pins. Also fixed: the last-synced time was Korean in every language.
