A step closer to the Bear that Android tablets never had.

• Keyboard shortcuts: Ctrl/Cmd+B, I and K for bold, italic and links on a hardware keyboard
• Tag autocomplete: type # in your note to reuse existing tags
• Table of contents: jump around long notes from the preview
• Serif font option in Settings for a book-like writing feel (code stays monospaced)
• Tablet 3-column layout: tap a tag in the sidebar to filter your notes in place

Your notes and highlights are untouched.
