Wording consistency. Twenty-four strings in Korean, French, German, Japanese and Spanish named one thing two ways inside a single screen, or broke a convention the rest of the app kept — an "archive" colliding with the Archive screen, an unpin verb sharing no stem with pin, mixed question mark widths, headings called titles. Release checks now catch a stale version number or an oversized store note before shipping, and the security notes say what the Locked space does and does not protect.
