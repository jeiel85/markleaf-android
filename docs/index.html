A real writing flow

The tools step back,
and the writing keeps flowing.

Open, write, organize, find, and export — all flowing naturally on a single device.

01 · Write

Syntax comes alive, and tools appear only when needed.

Your Markdown source stays intact while headings and emphasis render legibly as you edit. A small Aa button, selection-context actions, and the / command at the start of a line add formatting without cluttering the screen.

  • Live Markdown with CommonMark/GFM preview
  • 13 formatting actions and hardware-keyboard shortcuts
  • Quick Insert for tables, callouts, and wikilinks

02 · Organize & find

Tag, link, and search before you ever worry about folders.

A #tag in the body becomes your classification, and SQLite FTS search plus the Quick Switcher move you to the next thought fast, even across many notes.

  • Nested tags and per-tag note browsing
  • Wikilinks and backlinks
  • Pin, archive, and trash recovery

03 · Own & move

Leave files that stay readable outside the app.

Export all notes as standard Markdown, or mirror them to a SAF folder you choose. Even when you need to share a PDF, the original .md stays intact.

  • Single and all-note Markdown export
  • Mirror to a folder you choose
  • PDF and the Android share sheet

Privacy by architecture

Not sending anything
is the clearest protection.

Markleaf itself declares no internet permission. Notes, tags, attachments, and settings stay in app-private storage until you choose to move them yourself.

android.permission.INTERNET = false Read the full privacy policy

    Room DB and app-private attachment storage

  2. 2 You choose explicitly

    Export · Share · SAF folder

  3. 3 Moves along your path

    No Markleaf server or backend
