A small feature plus a fix. New optional setting "Open notes in preview" (Settings > Notes & search): with it on, notes open in the rendered preview and stay there as you browse, instead of the edit view. A brand-new note still opens for editing, and you can long-press the preview/edit icon to toggle it. Also fixed: right-to-left notes (Arabic, Hebrew, Persian) now lay out correctly in the editor and preview. Off by default.
