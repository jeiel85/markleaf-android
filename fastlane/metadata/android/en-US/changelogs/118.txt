Tap a checkbox in reading view to tick it off, instead of going back to the editor. Three fixes: a note whose body starts with a horizontal rule no longer loses the text after it; frontmatter written by other tools, including the indented tag lists Obsidian writes by default, now survives a sync unchanged; and settling a sync conflict no longer pushes a note you never edited to the top of your list. Saving also no longer gets slower the more notes you keep.
